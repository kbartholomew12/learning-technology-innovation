CASE STUDY 02 · SCALABLE LEARNING ARCHITECTURE

Scaling a Learning Model Without Losing Quality

How I connected learning architecture, faculty systems, operational planning, and continuous improvement to support growth from 4 sections on one campus to more than 70 sections across five campuses.

Growth should expand access without creating a different learning experience in every section. MY APPROACH TO SCALABLE LEARNING
The Challenge
From local success to scalable delivery

The Nutrition discipline was growing quickly, but expansion created a risk: as more sections, campuses, and faculty were added, the learner experience could become increasingly inconsistent.

The challenge was not simply to add classes. It was to build a repeatable model that could support growth while maintaining quality, preparing new faculty, and creating a more consistent experience across locations and delivery formats.
