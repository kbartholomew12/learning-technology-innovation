Aligning a new OER course to the evidence students must demonstrate

From textbook comparison through learning outcomes, formative practice, summative measures, and gap review.

The design challenge

The replacement of McGraw Hill's Essentials of Biology with OpenStax Concepts of Biology required more than matching chapter topics. I compared the resources against state-mandated outcomes and Valencia evidences of learning, developed aligned instruction and activities, and reviewed whether assessment evidence would cover the required expectations.

Two module crosswalk examples

Reconstructed from the 2026 BSC1005-OER project discussions, not a reproduction of the formal institutional crosswalk or exact approved learning-outcome wording.

Module 9

Meiosis Mission

Source alignment: Earlier Mader meiosis objectives and OpenStax Concepts of Biology §§7.1–7.3.

Learning evidence: Students work through chromosome identification, crossing over, meiosis I and II, mitosis comparison, and a nondisjunction case diagnosis.

Gap identified: Supplemental explanation was needed for XYY syndrome, which could otherwise have remained undercovered.

Module 11

Gene Expression Remix

Source alignment: Earlier Mader gene-expression objectives and OpenStax Concepts of Biology §§9.1–9.5.

Learning evidence: Students adjust gene-regulation conditions, compare outcomes, and explain consequences for protein production and cellular efficiency.

Gaps identified: Historical DNA-as-genetic-material experiments and explicit euchromatin/heterochromatin terminology needed targeted attention.

Illustrative outcome-to-evidence mapping

Module 9 · Meiosis

Learning expectation: Explain chromosome behavior and consequences of meiotic errors.

Content: OpenStax §§7.1–7.3, videos, and targeted supplement.

Formative activity: Meiosis Mission's scaffolded models and nondisjunction diagnosis, supported by knowledge checks.

Summative verification: Check approved common-exam coverage of the outcome; activity completion alone is not proof of complete summative coverage.

Module 11 · Gene Expression

Learning expectation: Trace DNA to RNA to protein and predict outcomes of gene regulation.

Content: OpenStax §§9.1–9.5 and supplemental explanations.

Formative activity: Gene Expression Remix simulation rounds, evidence-driven explanations, and a student report.

Summative verification: Confirm that approved shared assessments capture required interpretation and application, not simply recall.

Assessment distinction: Knowledge checks were formative. The project audit separately identified the need to verify approved shared exam/common summative assessment coverage. This page does not imply those checks are sufficient.

What this shows about responsible AI adoption

Coverage is not mastery

An OER chapter may discuss a topic without providing evidence that students can apply it. Alignment must connect learning outcomes, practice, feedback, and valid assessment.

Human academic review remains essential

The crosswalk process surfaced gaps and terminology mismatches. Those discoveries informed supplemental content and refinements rather than being hidden behind an apparently complete AI-generated mapping.
