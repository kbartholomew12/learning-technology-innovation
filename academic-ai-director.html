The decision framework

How I decide whether AI belongs in an academic experience

Define the learning: What should students know, do, or demonstrate? Choose the role of AI: Feedback, inquiry, critique, simulation, or faculty support? Protect the learning: Review scientific accuracy, bias, privacy, transparency, accessibility, and student agency. Measure value: Examine learning evidence and faculty experience before scaling.
